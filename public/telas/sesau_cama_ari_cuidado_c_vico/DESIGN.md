## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a welcoming, clear, and institutional visual language for the Municipal Secretariat of Health of Camaçari (SESAU / Bahia). Rooted in the visual identity of the municipal portal, the aesthetic blends **Institutional Modernism** with an **Empathetic Civic** design pattern. It replaces bureaucratic coldness with approachable, high-clarity public service interactions that inspire safety and confidence.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
- **Primary Audience:** Diverse municipal citizens of all age brackets, with a crucial focus on elderly residents, families, and individuals using entry-level mobile devices in bright ambient daylight to access primary care, medication stock, and healthcare appointments.
- **Emotional Response:** Clarity, human dignity, trust, and effortless orientation. Users should immediately feel guided and reassured when locating clinics, tracking vaccinations, or navigating emergency care (UPA and Pronto Atendimento).

### Design Style & Core Principles
- **Clarity over Complexity:** Clear visual hierarchies, high font contrast, unambiguous action buttons, and accessible typography.
- **Generous Ergonomics:** Touch targets are calibrated for easy tapping without accidental triggers.
- **Civic Accessibility (WCAG AAA Focus):** Color combinations, font sizes, and structural containers are calibrated to exceed contrast requirements across light backgrounds and responsive viewports.

## Colors

The color palette reflects the institutional visual identity of the Municipal Secretariat of Health of Camaçari, centered on the signature civic purple.

### Primary Palette (Institutional Purple)
- **Primary Base (`#7B357D` / `#8B3D8D`):** The primary color represents SESAU Camaçari. It drives headers, primary banners, category navigation, and key institutional landmarks.
- **Primary Dark & Deep (`#5A1E5C` / `#4A154D`):** High-contrast purple tones applied to text on tinted surfaces, navigation bars, interactive hover states, and critical headers.
- **Tonal Lavender Surfaces (`#F5E9F7` / `#FBF5FC`):** Soft, soothing background tints that separate content modules without introducing harsh gray dividers.

### Functional & Supporting Colors
- **Secondary (`#12824C` - Vitality Green):** Assigned to affirmative actions, confirmed vaccination cards, open health post indicators, and affirmative civic calls to action.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Guidance Amber):** Dedicated to attention notices, pending lab results, and service advisories.
- **Emergency (`#C5221F` - Pronto Atendimento / UPA Red):** Reserved exclusively for urgent healthcare, 24h emergency indicators, and critical health alerts.
- **Neutral Base (`#251728` - Deep Mulberry Slate):** Deep neutral tone providing maximum contrast (>10:1) on white and lavender surfaces for comfortable reading.
- **Canvas & Card Surfaces (`#FAF7FC` / `#FFFFFF`):** A bright canvas foundation designed to minimize outdoor glare while preserving crisp card borders.

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Public Sans**, an open, highly accessible civic typeface engineered for public sector interfaces, administrative clarity, and cross-platform consistency.

### Editorial & Inclusivity Guidelines
- **Legibility by Default:** Standard paragraph body text is set to `16px` (`body-md`), with critical instructions rendered at `18px` (`body-lg`) to aid reading speed and low-vision accessibility.
- **Glyph Distinction:** Public Sans clearly differentiates between numerals (`1`), uppercase letters (`I`), and lowercase characters (`l`), preventing mistakes in medical record numbers, CNS (Cartão Nacional de Saúde) fields, and protocol codes.
- **Proportional Line Spacing:** Line heights are maintained between 1.4x and 1.55x the font size to prevent visual crowding when users enlarge browser text.
- **Accessibility Controls:** The design system reserves top-bar slots for browser-level font resizing (`A+` / `A-`) and high-contrast switches.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system employs an accessible, modular structure that prioritizes single-column, tap-friendly navigation on mobile devices while expanding into organized multi-column panels on wider viewports.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (0 – 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Information cards, emergency units, and service shortcuts stack vertically in full-width rows to maintain an uninterrupted reading flow.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Service shortcuts (Vacinas, Remédios, Postos, Consultas) organize into 2-column or 4-column grids.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum container width of `1240px` with `2.5rem` outer canvas margins to maintain comfortable reading line lengths (50 to 75 characters per line).

### Touch Buffers & Rhythms
- Interactive controls maintain a minimum touch clearance of `8px` (`space-sm`) around hit targets, preventing misclicks when tapping adjacent items on mobile screens.

## Elevation & Depth

To prevent visual noise and preserve crisp reading clarity, this design system avoids heavy drop shadows. Depth is communicated through **Soft Tonal Layering combined with Low-Contrast Borders and Tinted Ambient Elevations**.

### Elevation Scale
- **Canvas (Level 0):** Flat background in `#FAF7FC` or clean `#FFFFFF`.
- **Card Surfaces (Level 1):** Solid white `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a clean `1.5px` border in `#E5D9E8`. A subtle ambient shadow adds depth: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(74, 21, 77, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(74, 21, 77, 0.03)`.
- **Interactive Elevated Cards (Level 2):** Focused cards and hovered action tiles lift gently: `box-shadow: 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(74, 21, 77, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(74, 21, 77, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Modals & Sheets (Level 3):** Urgent notices, filter panels, and dialog sheets rest atop a 45% dark purple scrim (`rgba(42, 12, 44, 0.45)`), using `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(74, 21, 77, 0.14), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(74, 21, 77, 0.08)`.

### Border Integrity
Every container maintains an outline boundary so that users with low screen contrast or high ambient sunlight can clearly distinguish separate cards and interactive regions.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates balanced, approachable geometry with friendly rounded corners that communicate human care while remaining structured and institutional.

### Shape Conventions
- **Inputs, Buttons, and Alerts:** Standard `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius for sharp readability and tactile affordance.
- **Service Cards, Health Post Units, and Hub Panels:** `1rem` (16px) corner radius (`rounded-lg`) offering a warm and inviting shape.
- **Top Bottom Sheets and Overlay Modals:** `1.5rem` (24px) top-corner radius (`rounded-xl`).
- **Filter Tags, Counter Bubbles, and Status Badges:** Fully circular/pill `9999px` radius, cleanly distinguishing metadata chips from primary rectangular action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Touch Target Dimensions:** Minimum height of `52px` on mobile (never below `48px`), accommodating users with varying motor control.
- **Primary Institutional Button:** Background `#7B357D`, hover `#5A1E5C`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight `600`, font size `17px`.
- **Vital Action Button (Secondary Green):** Background `#12824C`, hover `#0E663B`, text `#FFFFFF`, used for "Agendar Consulta", "Confirmar Presença", and "Solicitar Remédio".
- **Outlined Purple Button:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `2px solid #7B357D`, text `#7B357D`.
- **Emergency Button (Pronto Atendimento / UPA):** Background `#C5221F`, hover `#A31816`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Focus Indicator:** Continuous `3px` solid `#8B3D8D` ring with `2px` white offset space for accessible keyboard and assistive focus.

### Service Hub Cards (Vacinas, Postos, Remédios, Agendamento)
- **Visual Anatomy:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, `1.5px` border in `#E5D9E8`, `rounded-lg` corner radius, with internal padding of `1.25rem` (`20px`).
- **Institutional Top/Left Accent:** Feature cards can display a `4px` solid accent bar in `#7B357D` (primary) or `#12824C` (positive/health status) to aid rapid scanning.
- **Icon Container:** Circular or soft-square background in `#F5E9F7` housing icons rendered in `#7B357D`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Height & Spacing:** Minimum height of `52px`, vertical padding of `14px`, and horizontal padding of `16px`.
- **Structural Border:** `1.5px` solid `#BFA5C4` at rest, transitioning to `2px` solid `#7B357D` on active focus.
- **Labeling:** Persistent top-aligned labels (`label-md` in `#4A154D`) to ensure users do not lose context when typing.
- **Search Header Bar:** Integrated search inputs feature a prominent magnifying glass icon button with clear placeholder text (e.g., *"Buscar posto de saúde, remédio ou especialidade..."*).

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Dimensions:** Minimum vertical height of `32px` with horizontal padding of `12px` and pill geometry (`9999px`).
- **Variants:**
  - *Aberto / Disponível:* `#ECFDF5` background with `#065F34` text.
  - *Atenção / Documento Pendente:* `#FFFBEB` background with `#92400E` text.
  - *Plantão 24h / Urgência:* `#FDF2F2` background with `#991B1B` text.
  - *Informativo Geral:* `#F5E9F7` background with `#5A1E5C` text.

### Header & Navigation
- **Municipal Header Bar:** Top accessibility strip with high-contrast toggle, `A+ / A-` font scaling, and VLibras integration.
- **Hero Title Bar:** Signature `#7B357D` background displaying the official municipal seal, the SESAU heart-and-syringe civic insignia, clear institutional typography in pure `#FFFFFF`, and immediate direct contact channels (e.g., *"Telefone: 71 3621-6824"*).
- **Service Shortcuts (Quick Access):** Rapid navigation pills for *Unidades Básicas (UBS)*, *Vacinação*, *Farmácia Municipal*, *Consultas Especializadas*, and *Vigilância em Saúde*.
